# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Vellum Research a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate constructively and empathetically in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Respect differing viewpoints and focus on evidence-backed engineering decisions.
- Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers.
