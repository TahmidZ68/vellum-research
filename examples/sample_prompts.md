# Sample Research Prompts

Use these benchmark questions to test Vellum Research across business, technical, and empirical domains:

1. **Technical Framework Comparison (Recommended First Demo)**
   > Compare the leading open-source AI agent frameworks. Evaluate their architecture, developer experience, extensibility, deployment options, limitations, and suitable use cases. Provide a comparison table and cite primary documentation wherever possible.

2. **Regional Market Analysis**
   > Research the current AI automation market in Bangladesh. Identify major competitors, common services, pricing models, customer segments, and opportunities for a new entrant.

3. **Scientific / Empirical Evaluation**
   > Evaluate peer-reviewed evidence on the productivity impact of AI coding assistants in enterprise software engineering teams between 2023 and 2026, noting study methodologies, sample sizes, and conflicting findings.
